## Points
- APA press release dated 2026-09-03 names Muriel Clauson Closs, PhD, first executive director of the Center. Described as an industrial-organizational psychologist, formerly chief executive of Anthill. https://www.apa.org/news/press/releases/2026/09/closs-behavioral-science-ai
- Center aim: apply behavioral science to how AI is designed and how it affects society; work with leading technology companies on how AI systems are designed, evaluated and deployed. Same release and https://www.apa.org/news/press/releases/2026/09/arthur-evans-retirement
- APA CEO Arthur C. Evans Jr., PhD, announced in September 2026 that he will retire effective 2028-01-03 after a national search; the release credits him with leading creation of the Center. Same retirement release.
- APA board approved a Strategic Investment Fund request for the Center in February 2026, per a search summary of the minutes (amount not repeated here because the PDF was unreadable): https://www.apa.org/about/governance/board/february-may-2026.pdf
- March 2026 Monitor column by the CEO on AI and psychology; it does not name the Center: https://www.apa.org/monitor/2026/03/psychology-impacts-artificial-intelligence
