description: Optional: interview prep for an imported interview packet (modes: research, prep, gaps, debrief)
---
Use the `interview-prep` skill for: $ARGUMENTS. The first word may be a mode (research, prep, gaps, debrief) followed by the application slug; default is prep. Work from `kb/interviews/<slug>/packet.md` (import it first with `python scripts/import_interview_packet.py <packet.md>` if it is missing). Save interview-research.md (research) or interview-prep.md (prep) in `kb/interviews/<slug>/` and print it.
