# Resume Bullet Writer (career-pkm)

Original generic body replaced; see git history.

## Rules
- A bullet is the canonical wording of one KB claim (`kb/_views/claims.md`). Do not rewrite, merge, split or extend claims. Every bullet maps to a claim ID.
- Choose the starting verb from the claim's bracketed alternatives (`[A | B | C]`; first is the preferred draft). Unique starting verb within each role. Prefer the more conservative option ("primary" over "sole") and log the choice in `proposals.md`.
- Numbers only from the claim's `metrics`, copied in literal form (`6,000+`). Never add, estimate, round, convert to a range or derive a number. A claim without a number stays without one. If a number is missing from `metrics`, use a second claim whose metrics carry it, or pick another claim; record the gap in `proposals.md` for the owner.
- Never "find related metrics" or invent outcomes, scale or results. If a stronger bullet needs a fact the KB lacks, ask the owner and write it to `applications/<slug>/proposals.md`.
- Weak openers ("Responsible for", "Helped with", "Duties included") in canonical wording are reported to the owner, not fixed here.
- No em dashes. Follow `applications/preferences.md`.
