# Research: MAVEN Project (Program Coordinator)

Searches used only the employer name and role title from the JD. Facts below come from third-party search summaries, not from fetching MAVEN's own pages, so treat counts as approximate and unverified. Nothing here is a fact about the owner.

## Organization
- Independent 501(c)(3) that began as a project of medical school alumni association presidents. It connects providers in safety net clinics with expert physician volunteers for advice, mentorship and education ([MAVEN mission page](https://mavenproject.org/?p=1432), [ACP Internist, 2015](https://www.acpinternist.org/weekly/archives/2015/03/31/8.htm)).
- Volunteers are largely retired or semi-retired specialists. Figures differ by page and date (233 volunteers across 69 specialties on the mission page; "over 165" in a later partner press release). Use no number in application text.
- Telehealth is central: clinic providers present de-identified cases for asynchronous or immediate consults on a HIPAA-compliant platform ([Safety Net Connect partnership](https://www.01net.it/maven-project-and-safety-net-connect-partner-to-increase-equitable-access-to-specialty-care-in-marginalized-communities)). A Direct Relief partnership extended telehealth service to clinics nationally ([MAVEN and Direct Relief](https://mavenproject.org/direct-relief)).
- Education: CME and clinical mentoring alongside consults. The search results did not name ACCME or describe the accreditation mechanism, so the ACCME detail rests on the JD alone. Confirm on MAVEN's site before the interview.
