# Positioning map: APA, Center Coordinator (CBSAI)

Built with the competitive-cartographer method. Facts about the owner come only from the KB (claim IDs). The applicant field below is an assumption from the JD's wording, not research on real applicants; no web search was run on candidates.

## 1. The space
A founding-phase APA center (AI and behavioral science, first priority Youth Safety & Wellbeing) hiring one coordinator who is "at its core, a highly capable executive assistant and operations role" for an Executive Director and a Scientific Director. The JD asks for calendar and travel work, partner and funder pipelines, convenings, an annual calendar and a master tracker of external deadlines (`jd.md`).

## 2. Likely applicant archetypes (estimates, from the JD only)
| Archetype | Typical strength | Typical weakness against this JD |
|---|---|---|
| Career executive or administrative assistant | Calendar, travel booking, gatekeeping | Little behavioral science, policy or funder-pipeline context |
| Recent psychology or policy graduate | Domain interest, research exposure | Thin executive-support and convening logistics |
| Research or lab coordinator | "Research environment", senior academics | Less partner and funder pipeline work, less travel and event scale |
| Event or conference planner | Venue, catering, run-of-show | Less discretion-heavy executive support and deadline tracking |

## 4. Where the owner sits (KB evidence)
- Executive and operations depth: owned calendar and scheduling for senior executives, 20+ weekly touchpoints (A-ncmw-project-coordinator-25, -111); travel, air bookings and hotel accommodations for speakers (A-ncmw-project-coordinator-31); post-meeting notes and action tracking (A-ncmw-project-coordinator-18).
- Convenings: NatCon26 track, 18+ sessions, 6,000+ attendees, 15+ organizations (A-ncmw-project-coordinator-27, -105).
- Partner and funder pipeline: 50+ speakers and partner organizations recruited and onboarded (A-ncmw-project-coordinator-30, -115).
- Deadlines: milestones and reporting deadlines across 6+ concurrent workstreams, 100% on-time deliverable completion (A-ncmw-project-coordinator-02, -04, -104).
- Domain fluency, including AI: evaluated 5 edtech/AI vendor platforms against 30+ federal standards including NIST AI RMF (A-ncmw-project-coordinator-50, -87); behavioral health from direct care (Casa Serena claims above) through housing (Sanctuary Centers) to national association work.
- Research environment and academics: three concurrent labs, meeting agendas, minutes and action items (A-ucsb-research-assistant-01, -24).
- Start-up conditions: sole communications owner with no support staff (A-asei-marketing-specialist-01); standardizing workflows for a newly formed team (A-ncmw-project-coordinator-11).
- Discretion: confidentiality with sensitive client and participant information (A-sanctuary-centers-housing-coordinator-13, A-ucsb-research-assistant-05, A-casa-serena-program-assistant-20).

## 5. White space and recommended position
Intersection white space: an operations coordinator who has run convenings, pipelines and deadline trackers at national scale and understands both behavioral health practice and AI compliance. Most applicants will pick one side of the map.

Suggested one-line position (for the interview, not a new fact): *a coordinator who keeps leaders' calendars, partners and deadlines moving, with working knowledge of behavioral health and AI governance from inside it.*

Three differentiators to lead with, each tied to the JD: (1) convenings at national scale (NatCon26 claims); (2) pipelines and deadlines held to 100% on-time delivery across 6+ workstreams; (3) AI vendor evaluation against federal standards, a direct link to the Center's subject.

## 6. Honest weaknesses
- No executive assistant title and no travel-booking platform named in the KB.
- No founding of a new organization; start-up evidence is sole-owner and new-team work.
- No venue, catering or printed-materials claims; the KB convenings are mostly presenter and virtual logistics.
- No annual-calendar owner or master external-deadline tracker for a whole organization; closest are workstream trackers.
- The NCMW role ran Oct 2025 to May 2026, so the most relevant role is the shortest.
- The submitted resume repeated NCMW bullets under Casa Serena (see `match.md`); be ready to say it was a paste error and give the correct bullets.

## 7. Anti-patterns to avoid in the interview
- Do not claim to be an experienced executive assistant; position as an operator who has done the same work for leaders.
- Do not claim start-up founding experience; say what the KB shows.
- Do not give numbers beyond the claims listed above.
