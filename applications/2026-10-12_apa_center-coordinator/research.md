# Research: APA, Center Coordinator (Center for Behavioral Science and AI)

Searches used only the employer name, the center name and the role title from the JD. Facts below come from search summaries, not fetched pages, and are approximate and unverified. Nothing here is a fact about the owner.

## What the searches confirmed
- APA (American Psychological Association) published *Artificial Intelligence and Adolescent Well-being: An APA Health Advisory* in 2025, written by an expert advisory panel. It defines adolescence as ages 10 to 25 and urges AI developers to build protections against exploitation, manipulation and erosion of real-world relationships, for example periodic reminders that users are talking to a bot ([NPR, June 2025](https://www.npr.org/2025/06/04/nx-s1-5420770/a-new-health-advisory-calls-on-ai-developers-to-protect-young-people), [Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/articles/apa-calls-for-guardrails-education-to-protect-adolescent-ai-users)).
- An OpenAI page, dated August 2026, describes a partnership with APA to bring psychological science into responsible AI development for young people ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/mr-IN/index/openai-and-apa-partner-to-advance-responsible-ai/)). It is company-authored; treat the framing as OpenAI's.
- This is consistent with the JD: the Center's first priority area is Youth Safety & Wellbeing, and "AI-company and industry supporters" are among its partners.

## Not confirmed
- The Center for Behavioral Science and AI (CBSAI) by name did not appear in any result. Treat it as JD wording only.
- Names of the Executive Director and Scientific Director: not found. None are used anywhere in the application. Check APA's site and LinkedIn before an interview.
- Employee reviews: results were thin and mixed (a Glassdoor summary shows about 3.6 of 5 for culture and 4.2 for work-life balance, with one 2026 review calling the culture toxic). The results also mixed in other organizations abbreviated "APA", so the review data may not be APA's. Not relied on.
