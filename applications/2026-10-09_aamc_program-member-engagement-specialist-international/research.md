# Research: AAMC, Program and Member Engagement Specialist, International

Searches used only the employer name, the AAHCI program names, and the role title from the JD. Facts below come from third-party search summaries, not from fetching AAMC pages, so treat details as approximate and unverified. Nothing here is a fact about the owner.

## Organization
- The Association of American Medical Colleges is a not-for-profit association supporting medical education, medical research and patient care across its member institutions (JD).
- AAHCI (Alliance of Academic Health Centers International) is the international arm of the AAMC and a wholly owned subsidiary of it. It came into the AAMC in 2022 when the Association of Academic Health Centers and AAHCI merged into it ([AAMC AAHCI page](https://www.aamc.org/learn-network/affinity-groups/aahci-alliance-academic-health-centers-international), [merger announcement](https://www.aamc.org/news/press-releases/aamc-aahc-approve-merger-agreement)).
- Stated mission: to empower a global community of academic health center leaders to advance health and well-being by fostering collaboration and excellence. The AAMC says its membership includes more than 60 academic health centers outside North America (same AAMC page).
- Programs named in the JD that the search confirmed:
  - AAHCI Student Leadership Initiative (ASLI): invites health professions students at member institutions to propose ideas on a yearly theme. 2025 theme: artificial intelligence in health professions education. 2026 theme: innovative approaches to the future health workforce ([ASLI page](https://www.aamc.org/learn-network/affinity-groups/aahci/aahci-student-leadership-initiative-asli)).
  - AAHCI Leadership Perspectives: a regular series of commentaries from academic health center leaders worldwide ([publications page](https://aamc.org/career-development/affinity-groups/aahci/about-leadership-resources-and-publications)).
  - International Advisory Committee on Artificial Intelligence: set up in 2024 by the AAMC, AMEE, IAMSE, APMEN-Net and AAHCI to develop a worldwide vision for AI in medical education ([AAMC newsletter item](https://aamc.org/media/81911/download?attachment=)).
- Not confirmed by search: the "Global Engagement Forum" (the closest named convening is the Global Innovation Forum, held in Toronto in April 2026 and being renamed the AAHCI-AAMC Leadership Forum for February 2027; see [AAHCI meetings](https://www.aamc.org/learn-network/affinity-groups/aahci/meetings)), "AAHCI Connect" (the AAMC page mentions webinars and roundtables for members) and the "Global Workforce Advisory Panel". Treat these as JD wording and confirm with the recruiter.

## Hiring signals
- Remote-eligible in the contiguous U.S., excluding CA, MA, NY and OR; the owner's Dallas, TX location qualifies. Grade range $55,930 to $65,800 (JD). The role works with the AAHCI Senior Director, the AAHCI Senior Program Specialist and the Regional Offices.
- Preferred, not required: member association experience and international experience (JD). Required: a bachelor's degree in health systems administration, public health, global affairs, business or a related field, plus 1 to 2 years of related work.
- Third-party directories list names for AAHCI staff (an AAHCI Senior Director, a Senior Program Specialist, a Director of Meetings and Membership, and the executive director named at the 2022 merger). These listings may be out of date and I did not verify them, so no names are used in the letter. Check the AAHCI team on LinkedIn before an interview; a general contact is aahci@aamc.org per AAMC's AAHCI pages.
