## Organization
- United Way of Tarrant County (UWTC) is a Fort Worth area United Way chapter. Its careers page says it employs more than 70 people across its main operations and the Area Agency on Aging ([UWTC careers](https://www.unitedwaytarrant.org/careers)).
- Community investments are grants to local nonprofits, funded mainly through a Community Fund plus a Veterans Fund and a Women United Fund. Announcements: about $2.7M to 54 agencies in July 2022 ([Arlington press item](https://www.arlingtontx.gov/News-Articles/2022/July/United-Way-Tarrant-County-Invests-2.7M-Supports-Arlington-Nonprofits)); $2.1M for 66 programs in October 2023 ([UWTC release](https://www.unitedwaytarrant.org/blog/press-releases/united-way-tarrant-county-announces-21m-community-investment)).
- Focus areas named in those announcements: community response, community health, financial empowerment, and education and learning. Search summaries described the issues funded as affordable housing, educational resources, health care and transportation.
- Benevity profile description of the mission: identifies issues through research and data, informs the public about major social issues, invites community involvement, and invests in equitable solutions that produce long-term system change ([Benevity](https://causes.benevity.org/causes/840-750858360?lang=en)). This is a third-party summary, not the official mission statement. Confirm the official wording on unitedwaytarrant.org before quoting it.
- Mission United is UWTC's military and veterans outreach initiative, connecting veterans, active-duty members and families with resources (same search results). Veterans Fund launched in 2013 with a corporate gift from Lockheed Martin Aeronautics.
